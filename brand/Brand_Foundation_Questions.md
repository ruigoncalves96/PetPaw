# Brand Foundation — Discovery Questions

*Started 21 Nov 2026. Answer in PT or EN, short or long. Write your first answer from your gut. You can revise it later.*

**How to use this:** the ★ questions are the core. If you only have 20 minutes, answer those first. Anything you're unsure about, write "unsure" and why. That's useful too. Once it's filled in, Claude turns it into a **Brand Foundation** (purpose, promise, values, personality, voice, visual principles), which then drives the name, logo, typography and the palette choice.

What we already know (from the market study and the survey work): every species, shared care, almost no typing; Portugal first, PT + EN; freemium; PetNoter is the main competitor; working name "PetPaw"; 4 palette directions exist (Moss & Marigold recommended).

---

## 1. Origin — why does this exist?

1. ★ **What moment made you want to build this?** Describe a real situation: a missed vaccine, a sitter who didn't know the routine, a vet asking something you couldn't answer…
2. ★ **If this app disappeared in 3 years, what would people miss?** One sentence.
3. Is this personal for you? Which animals do you have now, and which have you had before?
4. What makes you angry or frustrated about how pet care works today: apps, vets, information, the industry?
5. Fill in: *"We exist so that every animal ______."*

## 2. Who we serve

6. ★ **Describe one real person you're building this for.** Age, animals, home, how they feel about their pets, what their day looks like.
7. Who comes first at launch? Rank:
   - [ ] dog/cat owners
   - [ ] multi-species households
   - [ ] exotic keepers (reptiles, birds, fish…)
   - [ ] horse owners
   - [ ] families sharing care
   - [ ] sitters/walkers
8. ★ **How do you want them to *feel* after using the app for 30 seconds?** Pick 3: *calm, in control, proud, relieved, organised, cared for, smart, connected, reassured, delighted, unworried*… or your own words.
9. And how do they feel *before* the app, when they're stressed or worried?
10. Are pets "family members", "animals in our care", or "companions"? Which word fits your users, and which word would irritate them?
11. Who is **not** our user? Breeders, shelters, vets as customers, people who want a social network for pets…?

## 3. What we believe — values and stance

12. ★ **Pick 3–4 values we would defend even when it costs us money.** Examples: *every animal matters equally · honesty over hype · privacy (your data is yours) · simplicity over features · science/vet-backed · kindness · reliability*.
13. What will we **never** do? E.g. sell user data, push products, use dark-pattern paywalls, guilt-trip owners, show ads…
14. How do we treat **exotic animals**: equal citizens, or "also supported"? This choice shows everywhere: icons, illustrations, copy.
15. What's our stance on **vets**? Partner, authority we defer to, or one caretaker among many?
16. When someone's animal dies, what does the app do? (This one question shows a lot about the brand's soul.)

## 4. Personality and voice

17. ★ **If the brand were a person, who would it be?** Their job, age, how they dress, how they talk. (E.g. *"a calm vet nurse who remembers every patient's name"* vs *"a fun, slightly nerdy friend who owns six animals"*.)
18. ★ **Place us on each scale** (mark 1–5):

    | 1 | | 3 | | 5 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Playful | | | | Serious |
    | Warm | | | | Clinical |
    | Cute | | | | Sophisticated |
    | Bold / loud | | | | Quiet / calm |
    | Expert | | | | Friendly peer |
    | Modern / techy | | | | Natural / organic |
    | Minimal | | | | Rich / illustrated |
    | Local (Portuguese) | | | | Global |

19. Should the app use humour? If yes, what kind: gentle puns, animal jokes, or none in health moments?
20. Write the push notification for a vaccine due tomorrow, in the tone you'd *love* to receive.
21. Should we talk *to the owner* or *as/for the animal*? ("Luna's vaccine is due" vs "Luna says: time for my jab! 🐾")
22. "Tu" or "você" in Portuguese? Formal or informal?

## 5. Look and feel

23. ★ **Name 3 brands (any industry) whose *feeling* you'd like to borrow, and what exactly you like about each.** E.g. Headspace (calm), Duolingo (character), Monzo (clarity), Aesop (craft), Patagonia (values).
24. Name 1–2 brands or apps whose look you **don't** want to resemble. Why?
25. ★ **Palette:** from round 1 (Moss & Marigold / Lagoon & Coral / Plum & Apricot / Cobalt & Sun), which 1–2 feel like *us*, and why?
26. Illustrations, photos, or neither? If illustration: realistic, flat, hand-drawn, or abstract?
27. A **mascot or character**: yes, no, maybe later? If yes, which animal, or a non-species creature so no animal is "the main one"?
28. Should the paw be part of the identity, or is it too generic (every pet app uses it)?
29. Should the app feel like a **medical record** (trust, precision), a **diary/journal** (memories, warmth), or a **home dashboard** (control, calm)? Pick one, or rank them.

## 6. Position — how we're different

30. ★ **Finish this:** *"For ______ who ______, [Brand] is the ______ that ______. Unlike PetNoter, we ______."*
31. What's the one thing a user should tell a friend about us? (Word of mouth in ≤10 words.)
32. Is "Portuguese-made" part of the story or invisible? Should we lead in Portugal and *feel* global, or the other way round?
33. Premium tier: does it feel like a *treat* (extra delight), a *tool* (power features), or *support* (helping the project)? This affects how we write the paywall.

## 7. The name

34. ★ **Must the name work in both PT and EN?** Easy to say in both, no bad meaning in either?
35. Which style do you prefer?
    - [ ] descriptive (PetPaw, PetCare…)
    - [ ] invented word (Kova, Lumi…)
    - [ ] real word with a twist (Herd, Nest, Burrow…)
    - [ ] Portuguese-rooted (Ninho, Bicho…)
    - [ ] a person's or animal's name
36. Should the name hint at **dogs/cats**, at **all animals**, or at **caring/home**, without naming animals at all?
37. Are you attached to "PetPaw"? What do you like and dislike about it?
38. Do you need the .com, the .pt, or just the App Store name?

## 8. Ambition and limits

39. ★ **Where is this brand in 5 years?** A beloved Portuguese indie app, a European leader, a platform vets use, or something else?
40. If Phase 5 happens (community, marketplace, adoption), should the brand stretch to fit it, or stay a focused "care record"?
41. Will you appear as the founder (personal story, your animals in the marketing), or stay behind the brand?
42. Budget and time for brand work: DIY with Claude, a freelance designer for the logo, or an agency later?
