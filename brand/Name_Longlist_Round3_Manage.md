# Name Longlist — Round 3: "manage / control / keep"

*23 Nov 2026. Theme: names that carry the idea of managing, keeping in hand, steering, holding it all together. Same sound brief as before (soft, round, ends in a vowel, works in many languages, no "pet", no "paw"). See [Brand_Foundation.md §9](Brand_Foundation.md), [Name_Review_Round1.md](Name_Review_Round1.md), [Name_Longlist_Round2.md](Name_Longlist_Round2.md).*

*The conflict checks are quick web and App Store searches. They match by similarity, so "none found" is a hint, not proof. They are **not** a trademark search.*

## One tension to decide first

A name that literally says *control* or *manage* works against what you told us you want. You scored the brand **Warm = 1** and **Quiet = 4**, and the feelings you want are calm, relieved and reassured, not "in command". A cold, powerful name would also clash with the kindness value.

So the best names in this round say control **gently**: a hand that holds, someone who watches over, a light touch on the helm, everyone together. If you want "manage" said out loud, it can live in the tagline ("Everything about every animal, in one hand") instead of in the name.

## The four families

### 1. The hand (*manage* literally comes from Latin *manus*, "hand")
Italian *maneggiare*, "to handle", first meant handling and training **horses**. So "manage" has animals in its roots.

| Name | Say it | Story | Found |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manu** | MAH-noo | "Hand" (Lat. *manus*). Māori and Hawaiian: "bird". Sanskrit: Manu, the first lawgiver. | No app named Manu. But searches for "Manu + pet app" return **PetNoter**, because its developer is called Manu Benjamin. That would muddy search results against our main competitor. |
| **Mano** | MAH-noo | "Hand" in ES/IT. | No pet app found. In Brazilian Portuguese *mano* is slang for "bro", so it sounds casual and masculine. |

### 4. Everything together (the whole household in one place)

| Name | Say it | Story | Found |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tutti** | TOO-tee | Italian "all, everyone". In music, *tutti* means the whole orchestra playing together. | No app found. In Finnish *tutti* means a baby's pacifier (cute, but babyish). Playful, so it leans more "cute" than the quiet tone you asked for. |

## My pick and runner-up

1. **Vela**: the richest meaning of anything we've found. A candle kept burning ties to "never forgotten" and to the memorial. The sail is steering. *Velar* is watching over. All three are gentle, none is controlling. It also **replaces Velia** from round 2: same root, shorter and stronger. The one real risk is VelaVision.
2. **Tutti**: the warmest, most "everyone in the family" option. Check that it isn't too cute.

Manu has a good story but the PetNoter coincidence makes it a risky choice.

## Where this leaves the shortlist

Full list so far, across rounds 2 and 3: **Omia, Nolia, Vela, Noa, Mieli, Arca, Abri, Nidi, Tutti**. That's 9, so cut it to about 8 before testing. Velia is replaced by Vela.
