## 1. The soul: "No animal forgotten"

Your answers keep coming back to one idea: **forgetting**. It links everything in the project:

- **The origin.** The idea started as an app to find **lost** pets.
- **The everyday problem.** Owners *"forget a lot of valuable information"*, and what they do keep is scattered across apps.
- **The market.** Apps favour dogs and cats, so exotic animals are the ones left out.
- **The end of life.** The memorial: when an animal dies, it is still **remembered**.

So the soul isn't "pet records". It's **remembering, on behalf of people who love their animals**: every vaccine, every routine, every species, every life. Records, reminders, shared care and the memorial all follow from that.

**Purpose (recommended).** This merges your three versions:
> *We exist so that every animal gets the care it deserves and is never forgotten.*

**⚑ Tagline candidates** (to test once the name is chosen):
- *Every animal, remembered.*
- *All of them. All in one place.*
- *Care for every one of them.*

## 2. Who we're for

**Core person.** 20s–50s. Lives in a house with a garden, with a dog, two cats and maybe a reptile. Mornings start with feeding everyone, a dog walk, cuddles for the cats and a check on the terrarium, and evenings repeat it. They love these animals and try to give them the best they can, but keeping track of it all is stressful.

**Before → after.**

| Before | After 30 seconds in the app |
|---|---|
| Stressed, scattered, worried they've forgotten something | Calm, relieved, organised, reassured, unworried |

**How we talk about animals:** as **family members**. Never as "just animals", livestock or "units".

**Launch order vs. brand scope.** We *launch* with dog and cat owners (then exotic keepers, multi-species homes, horses, families, sitters). But the **brand must never look dog/cat-only**. Every icon, illustration and example should treat a gecko or a parrot the same as a dog. Dogs and cats get us in the door; all species equally is why we stand out.

**Long term.** A professional version for vets, breeders, shelters and farmers. That's one more reason the name can't contain "pet": a farmer's animals aren't pets, but they all need care.

## 3. Values: what each one means in practice

| Value | In practice we… | So we never… |
|---|---|---|
| **Every animal matters equally** | Design species templates with the same depth for a snake as for a dog. Use mixed species in every screenshot and illustration. | Default to a dog icon, use paw graphics, or label anything "other animals". |
| **Privacy** | Host in the EU, collect only what we need, offer easy export and deletion, explain data use in plain words. | Sell or share user data, or train third-party models on users' animals. |
| **Honesty over hype** | Make clear claims, show real screenshots and set honest prices. Say "we don't do that yet" when we don't. | Use fake urgency, fake reviews, AI-slop imagery or vague "AI-powered" bragging. |
| **Easy flow** | Show one thing at a time, keep typing close to zero, and make the next step obvious. | Pile features onto one screen or bury the important things. |
| **Kindness** | Speak gently, especially about health and death, and assume the owner is doing their best. | Guilt-trip ("You forgot Nala's vaccine!") or push products. |

*Five values is at the upper limit. If it ever needs trimming, "Easy flow" can fold into Kindness ("kind to your time").*

## 4. Positioning

**Positioning statement** (a refined version of your answer):
> **For** people who treat their animals as family, whatever the species, **[Brand]** is the calm home for everything about their care, shared with everyone who helps. **Unlike** PetNoter and other generic pet apps, it's built with the care, polish and species knowledge a family member deserves.

**Three reasons to choose us:**
1. **Every species, done properly.** Care adapts to each animal.
2. **Made for the whole family.** Every animal in it, and every person who helps look after them.
3. **Calm and beautiful to use.** Modern and well organised, with almost no typing. This answers your main complaint that current apps *"don't look good"* and are *"behind"*.

**Word of mouth (≤10 words).** Your "you can have everything in it!" is right, but "everything" can sound cluttered, which works against easy flow. Suggested version:
> *"All my animals in one app, and it's beautiful."*

**Made in Portugal:** a nice detail for the About page and founder story, but not part of the brand's image for now. The brand should feel global from day one.

## 5. Personality

**The person:** a calm, warm, gentle vet nurse who loves animals, knows every one of them and takes care of them gently. She talks to you **as a friend**, not as an authority.

**Your scores:**

| Scale | Score | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Playful ↔ Serious | **2** | Lightly playful. Smiles, but never clowns. |
| Warm ↔ Clinical | **1** | As warm as possible. Nothing cold or hospital-like. |
| Cute ↔ Sophisticated | **2** | Sweet and soft, but crafted rather than childish. |
| Bold ↔ Quiet | **4** | Quiet confidence. No shouting, no loud colours. |
| Expert ↔ Friendly peer | **5** | A friend who happens to know things. |
| Modern/techy ↔ Natural/organic | **5** | Natural, earthy and alive. |
| Minimal ↔ Rich | **1** | Very minimal. Lots of space, little decoration. |
| Local ↔ Global | **5** | Global. No regional flavour in the core identity. |

**Archetype:** *The Caregiver*, with a touch of *The Innocent* (sweetness, optimism).

**Three tensions, and how we resolve them:**
- **Vet nurse vs. friendly peer.** She *knows*, but *shares* rather than instructs: "Bearded dragons usually shed every 4–6 weeks", never "You must…". The expertise is in the content and the tone stays friendly.
- **Minimal vs. illustrations.** The UI is minimal. Illustrations are rare, small and meaningful (empty states, milestones, the memorial), not decoration.
- **Natural (5) vs. loving Arc/Dia.** What we take from Arc/Dia is their *craft*: smooth motion, human copy, soft colour and delightful details. We apply it to *natural* shapes and textures. The result is modern in how it's made and organic in how it feels.

## 6. Voice

**Rules**
1. **Talk to the person, and put the pet's name first.** "Nala's vet visit is tomorrow." The name always comes before the task. *(Answers 4.21: we don't speak as the animal by default. It quickly becomes cutesy, and it's wrong in health and memorial moments. If people want it, it could be a playful opt-in later.)*
2. **Gentle smile in routine moments, plain and calm in health moments.** At most one emoji in routine messages, and none in health warnings, errors or the memorial.
3. **Never guilt.** Overdue means "let's sort it", never "you forgot".
4. **Short, warm, concrete.** Give the time, the place and one clear action.
5. **Portuguese: "tu".** *(Answers 4.22, ⚑ confirm.)* "Você" can sound distant or awkward in PT-PT, and "tu" fits "friendly peer = 5" and an audience in their 20s–50s. In sensitive moments, impersonal phrasing works well.

**Examples**

| Moment | We say | We don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Routine reminder | Nala's vet visit is tomorrow at 10:00 🙂 · *A consulta da Nala é amanhã às 10:00 🙂* | Reminder: appointment due!!! |
| Overdue | Nala's vaccine is 3 days late. Book it or mark it as done? · *A vacina da Nala está atrasada 3 dias. Queres marcar ou registar como feita?* | You forgot Nala's vaccine! |
| Daily log | Breakfast for everyone ✓ | Feeding event logged successfully. |
| Empty state | Who's in your family? Add your first animal, any species. · *Quem faz parte da tua família? Adiciona o teu primeiro animal, de qualquer espécie.* | Add your first pet 🐶 |
| Error | Something went wrong on our side. Your data is safe. Try again in a moment. | Error 500. |
| Memorial | Luna will always be part of your family. Her reminders are paused, and her story is kept safe. · *A Luna fará sempre parte da tua família. Os lembretes foram pausados e a história dela fica guardada.* | Pet deleted. |
| Premium | Premium adds more for busy households. Everything essential stays free. | Unlock now before it's too late! |

**Word list**
- **Use:** your animals, family, care, everyone, the animal's name, "look after".
- **Avoid:** fur baby, pawrent, paw puns (not every animal has paws), "owner" in UI copy (prefer "you"), "units", "AI-powered" as a selling point.

## 7. Visual principles

1. **Calm and minimal.** Generous space, one focus per screen, few colours at a time.
2. **Soft and round.** Rounded corners, soft shapes and gentle motion, matching the round-sounding name we want.
3. **Natural warmth.** An organic, living feel, executed with modern polish.
4. **Every species, drawn equally.** One consistent illustration and icon style for all animals. No paw, no bone, no default dog.
5. **The animal is the hero.** The pet's photo and name lead every screen. The app is part **home dashboard** (today, what's next, everyone at a glance) and part **diary** (moments, history, memories).
6. **Health moments get serious.** Clear hierarchy, plain colours for status, no decoration.

**References:** Arc / Dia browser for craft, motion and human copy.
**Avoid:** Goodreads (dated), PetNoter (AI-slop imagery), stock photos of golden retrievers, neon "tech" gradients, clinical blue-and-white.

**Colour.** Not decided. The palette will be explored later, as its own round.

**Typography direction (round 2).** A humanist sans with soft, rounded details for the UI. It should be friendly but never bubbly, with strong PT accents and good legibility at small sizes (to test: Figtree, Nunito Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, DM Sans). Optionally, a soft serif for diary and memorial moments only (e.g. Fraunces at low "wonk").

**Illustration.** Flat or lightly hand-drawn, used sparingly, with one consistent line and fill style across all species.

**Mascot.** Not for now. A single animal mascot would break "every animal matters equally". If we ever want a character, it should be a non-species creature, decided after launch.

**A symbol to replace the paw** (explored in the logo round, once there's a name):
- **Nest / home.** A soft enclosing shape (care, home, the household).
- **Pebble family.** Rounded shapes that can each hold a different animal silhouette.
- **Trail of moments.** A gentle line of dots or marks (diary, timeline, never forgotten).

## 8. Moments that define us

- **When an animal dies: stop reminding, start remembering.** All reminders pause immediately and on their own. The profile moves to a **memorial** space (gentle wording, photos, the animal's story). It is never deleted unless the user asks, and we show no upsells there, ever. Sharing a memory with the family is optional.
- **Health alerts.** Calm, specific and actionable: no emoji, no jokes, no red panic screens.
- **The paywall** *(answers 6.33)*. Frame premium as **"more for your household"** (a tool) plus **"support the people who build it"** (honesty, founders visible). Never paywall the things that keep an animal safe: basic reminders, records and sharing stay free. This also turns one of PetNoter's main complaints into a strength for us.
- **Onboarding.** "Who's in your family?" Every species shows up from the first screen.
- **The founders.** Three friends who love animals, with their own animals in the story. Being real people supports "honesty over hype".

## 9. Naming brief

**Must have**
- A warm, soft, **round-sounding** word that makes people curious to learn what it is
- Easy to say and spell in PT and EN, and also ES / FR / DE / IT, since the ambition is global
- No bad or awkward meaning in any of those languages
- No "pet", no "paw", no single species
- Stretches to a future pro version (vets, breeders, farmers)
- Free on the App Store / Play Store and clear of trademarks (EUIPO + INPI, classes 9 / 42 / 44)

**Style:** an invented word, a real word with a twist, a Portuguese-rooted word or a name (anything except descriptive). It can refer to animals, care, home, or none of these.
**Not needed:** a .com or .pt domain. The store name is enough for now.

**Process (next round):** longlist about 40 → sound and meaning check in 6 languages → founders shortlist 8 → store and trademark check → test the top 3 with 10 people.
